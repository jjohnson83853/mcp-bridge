A request exceeding the budget is a deterministic pre-dispatch invalid_arguments rejection (reason `not_supported`). The MAXIMAL LEGAL REQUEST is the largest fields array satisfying BOTH the count/length/alphabet bounds AND the derived budget (computed, never assumed). Unknown-field rejection requires a non-empty list whose items all lack a requested field.

## 4. DESIGN
Selected approach (unchanged in kind): pure field-projection engine in NEW package-private `internal/service/field_projection.go`, invoked from TWO call sites — `callTool` at the decodeUpstreamResult call site (tools.go:1000) and `boundDirectExecution.call` (direct_execution.go:30) between its decode (:53) and result construction (:60-64) — in both cases before `t.boundedCallToolResult` (direct call site :65).
- Single-transformed-result rule (F1/R6-2, both paths): APPLY → one transformed result feeds every downstream derivation; SKIP/pass-through → original bytes/value flow unchanged (including any upstream reserved member, passed verbatim per the F1 authority rule).
- NORMATIVE DISCLOSURE SCHEMA (R6-1/R7-4/F3, both carriers): `{v:1, applied, entry, mode, fields, reason?}`, reason required iff applied=false; `fields` = applied projection's field list, `[]` when nothing applied/selected; `entry` = C1 entryID on matched rows, "" when no entry matched; `v` fixed 1; reason ∈ the closed 12-value enum.
- NORMATIVE D-TABLE (F-B, 21 rows, per-row outcome/reason/channel; P = payload member written by the bridge on the row's path, M = render metadata from the out-of-band threaded decision whenever a preview/page/terminal render occurs (budget permitting; default bounds: always for legal requests), L = one log record, C = counter {applied, skipped, rejected}):
  1. no match | absent/empty | — | — | pass-through | — | NONE (byte-identical)
  2. no match | full | — | no error | pass-through | full_requested | P (entry:"") + C(skipped)
  3. no match | full | — | error | pass-through | upstream_error | P (entry:"") + C(skipped)
  4. no match | fields | — | — | REJECT pre-dispatch | not_supported | L + C(rejected)
  5. match | absent | select absent | no error | shape ok | APPLY | — | P (entry=entryID) + C(applied)
  6. match | absent | select absent | no error | carrier failure — non-list | unparseable wrapper | non-mirrorable payload-bearing SC | ANY non-object item (WHOLE-LIST skip) | SKIP | payload_unparseable | payload_shape_unexpected | structured_content_not_mirrorable | item_shape_unexpected (per failure) | P + L + C(skipped)
  7. match | absent | select absent | no error | NON-EMPTY list, EVERY item misses ALL default fields | SKIP | item_shape_unexpected | P + L + C(skipped)
  8. match | absent | select absent | error | — | pass-through unchanged | — | NONE (AC6(a))
  9. match | absent | select present | no error | — | SKIP | upstream_select_present | P + L + C(skipped)
  10. match | absent | select present | error | — | pass-through unchanged | — | NONE (AC6(a))
  11. match | full | — | no error | — | pass-through | full_requested | P (entry=entryID) + C(skipped)
  12. match | full | — | error | — | pass-through | upstream_error (N6) | P (entry=entryID) + C(skipped)
  13. match | fields | select absent | no error | EMPTY list | APPLY | — | P + C(applied)

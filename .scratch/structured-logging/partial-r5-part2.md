Operator-facing order: (1) merge and build; (2) update repository docs/queries — `README.md`, `docs/logging-contract.md`, and `CHANGELOG.md` in the SAME change (T7, AC07-8, N4); (3) deploy. Not applicable beyond this: no checkpoint/restart of running bridges is required beyond the normal process restart that ships the new binary.

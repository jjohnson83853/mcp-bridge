## 8. RELEASE AND RECOVERY

- **PRE-RENAME failures: the binding STAYS RETIRED; the previous binary hits the ORIGINAL refusal (`reconcile.go:80-81`) — the crash-loop RESUMES until the new binary retries successfully or the owner lane repairs the state; retries are idempotent; the eventual successful retry emits exactly one record per reinstated server.**
- **POST-RENAME/FSYNC failure (ADR15): rejected admission, no record, on-disk state UNCERTAIN. Rollback compatibility DEPENDS ON THE SURVIVED BRANCH: survived — the previous binary reads/validates the non-retired snapshot and starts without crash-loop (startup: empty catalog until first probe; reload with successful discovery: current-era rows restored via the OLD binary's startup `definitionsFromSnapshot` path, `:901`); not survived — the tombstone remains and rollback resumes the crash-loop.
